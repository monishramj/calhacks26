### Contract choices these tests make (for G0 REVIEW and ws/web)

1. **IDs:** `^[A-Za-z0-9][A-Za-z0-9._:-]{0,127}$`. **Timestamps:** RFC 3339 with `Z` or a ±hh:mm offset, kept as sent.
2. **Report** = `{reportId, receiveSequence, receivedAt, provenance, input: ReportInput}`, the nested shape ws/web proposed. But `provenance` is `live | simulated | recorded | fixture` (AGENTS labels), not a model `Provider`.
3. **ReportInput combinations:**
   - `radio` ⇒ `verified` and `measured`, with a real encoding.
   - Non-radio ⇒ `not_applicable` verification.
   - `operator` ⇒ no encoding.
   - Byte counts are null exactly when `measurementSource` is `not_applicable`.
4. **LocalSuggestion:**
   - `NEW_INCIDENT` ⇒ no candidate.
   - `ATTACH_REPORT` / `FLAG_*` ⇒ a candidate that also appears in `baseIncidentVersions`.
   - `provider` ∈ local_laya / recording / fixture.
   - **IncidentProposal:** `baseIncidentVersions` includes `incidentId` (0 = new incident). No self-relations. A summary needs evidence. `provider` ∈ claude / operator / recording / fixture.
5. **Stored records:**
   - `SuggestionRecord {suggestionId, jobId, reviewState, suggestion}` and `ProposalRecord {jobId, submittedByRole, reviewState, proposal}`, both nested.
   - ws/web proposed a flat `IncidentProposal & {reviewState}`; REVIEW settles this with ws/web.
   - `reviewState` ∈ PENDING / APPROVED / REJECTED / STALE.
6. **ReviewEvent** `actorRole` is always `operator`, plus the backend `actorIdentity`. A manual change needs a reason or source reports.
7. **ScenarioState** exposes counts and the gate only. It has no next text and **no `nextMessageId`** (ws/web proposed one), so the client never sees future script content.
8. **Commands:**
   - `approveProposal` carries the `inputSignature` and `baseIncidentVersions` the operator saw.
   - `claimJob` has no `workerId`, because identity comes from the connection.
   - `advanceScenario` carries `expectedDeliveredCount`, so a double click can't deliver two reports.
   - `resetRun` carries `commandId` and `runId`.
9. **Roles:** operator / bridge / simulator / worker. Claude runs under the worker identity. Live and replay provider allow-lists are as listed in `permissions.test.ts`.
10. **Exercise fixture shape:** `{scriptVersion, places[{placeId, position{x,z}, aliases, subareas[{subareaId, aliases}]}], startingState{afterMessageId, provenance:'authored', incidents[...]}, events[{position, gate, gateRequiresIncident, radioCandidate, input}]}`. Expectations go in a separate `expectations.json` (`private`, `neverModelInput`, `interpretations`, `afterR07Confirmed`, `invariants`).
