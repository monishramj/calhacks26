### Test-design choices for review

- **Empty and whitespace-only text is rejected at the sender.** §20 allows "exact or explicit rejection". A report with no content carries nothing for the incident desk.
- **Rejection reasons name the field** (`method`, `type`, `utf`). That is a requirement on `parse_packet`'s existing `reason` string, not a new contract field.
- A forced method may refuse text it can't carry. Auto selection may not, because raw always works up to the size limit.

### Not covered here (belongs to G2 or later)

- The direct-message size ceiling and message-ID/version framing: G2 (preflight probe in `work/status/codec-radio.md`).
- Cross-machine decode on the actual two endpoints: G1 REVIEW.
- Windows runtime: the Windows build and a compiler-free Unishox2 come in G1 IMPLEMENT/REVIEW. `unishox2-py3` has no Windows wheels.
