**Exactly what Laya returns** (`Agent.system_one(state, questions)` → `{"answers": {qid: ...}}`):

- `choice`: `choice` (argmax key) and `probabilities` over **exactly the option keys asked**, softmax after per-option-count temperature, rounded to 4 places, sum 1. → `LabelScore.kind: 'probability'`.
- `score`: `score`, the expected ordinal Σ i·p_i over levels `0..n-1` (fractional, not a percentage), plus per-level `probabilities` and a `legend`. → `kind: 'score'`.
- `noul`: `noul` = P(true). → `kind: 'probability'`.
- Every answer also carries `confidence` (normalized entropy for choice/score, max(p) for noul), `answer_confidence` (max p), and `action.act_probability`. None of these are urgency, and none become scores.
- **The SDK silently truncates.** State past the room is right-truncated (`laya/common.py` `build_sequence`: `state_ids[:room]`), and the question header is trimmed to fit the options. The worker must count tokens and send oversize jobs to review instead of losing a correction.
- **Calibration warning at load:** "this checkpoint ships invalid temperatures … choice:11+ … → 0.5. Treat confidence from the affected entries as uncalibrated." Choice questions with 11 or more options are uncalibrated, so the candidate list per question must stay at 10 or fewer.
- Toy sample (authored, not exercise): "Open incident A: library basement burst pipe. New report: A family of three is stuck on the roof of the bakery as water rises." → action NEW_INCIDENT 0.987; attention POSSIBLE_LIFE_SAFETY 0.553 / INFORMATION 0.330 / ACTIVE_NEED 0.117; noul "someone may still be in danger" 0.365. This is one toy case. Flyby measured chance-level accuracy (0.25) on its own hand cases, so no quality claim is made here.

### Test-design choices for review

- Option keys asked of Laya are the §17 labels themselves, or candidate incident IDs, so `LabelScore.label` preserves the model's own labels. Candidate lists stay at 10 or fewer options (calibration warning above).
- "Low" and "flat" in the safety tests are clear-cut distributions (equal weights; 0.55 vs 0.40), so IMPLEMENT picks the review threshold without the tests pinning one.
- Uncertain attention → `attention: null` (review), never a default. Malformed output → REQUEST_REVIEW. Unavailable model → error, never a suggestion.
- Label accuracy on dev cases is evaluation and will be reported, not asserted. **Held-out cases:** §20 G4 asks for 24 or more, but `fixtures/private/heldout/` belongs to the PM and the inference workstream never authors or reads it (AGENTS.md). Not opened.
